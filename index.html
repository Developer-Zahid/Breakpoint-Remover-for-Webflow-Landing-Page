The problem

Once a breakpoint is in, it's in.

Add an XXL, XL or Large breakpoint — or inherit a few from a template or a copy-paste — and Webflow offers no button to delete it. The stray media queries ship to your published CSS and quietly break wide-screen layouts.

"I really do not need them, how to kill them?" — a recurring question across the Webflow forum
How it works

One panel. Full control over every large breakpoint.

Open the extension in the Designer and it reads your project live — every large breakpoint, widest first, with the exact number of styles on each.

See every breakpoint

1920px, 1440px and 1280px and above, each with its live style count and a badge for the one you're currently viewing.

Delete styles, or keep them

Remove a breakpoint and its styles for good, or drop it from the UI and keep the styles on your stylesheet — they return if you re-add the breakpoint.

Switches the canvas for you

If you're viewing the breakpoint you remove, it moves the canvas back to Desktop first, so you're never left on a view that no longer exists.

Clear confirmation

Every removal asks first, then reports exactly what happened — which breakpoint went and how many styles were deleted.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can you remove custom breakpoints in Webflow?
Not with Webflow's built-in settings. Once you add the XXL, XL or Large breakpoint, Webflow gives no option to remove it. Breakpoint Remover for Webflow adds that option inside the Designer.
Will my styles be deleted?
Your choice. Pick "delete" to remove the breakpoint and its styles for good, or "keep" to remove only the breakpoint from the Designer while the styles stay on your stylesheet — they come back if you re-add the breakpoint.
Does it affect Tablet or Mobile breakpoints?
No. It only removes the larger breakpoints (1280px and up). Desktop, Tablet and Mobile are never touched.
Do I need to publish afterwards?
Yes. Publish the site to update the live CSS so it matches what you see in the Designer.
Is my data safe?
Yes. The extension works only on Webflow Designer pages and doesn't collect, store or send any data — no analytics, no tracking.
